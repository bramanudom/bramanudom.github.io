A few of our favorite places to eat, shop, and explore around Chiang Mai.

Food & Drink

  • Chang Moi Ice Syrup Stall

    Pet's pick

    Pet's absolute favorite Thai dessert stall. Running since Pet's mom was a child, now managed by the next generation. Order the shaved ice with red syrup, grass jelly, and condensed milk!
