Accommodations

Our ceremony and reception will be at the Anantara Chiang Mai Resort, and we've arranged a group rate there if you'd like to stay on-site with us. That said, please don't feel any pressure to stay at the venue—Chiang Mai is compact and easy to get around (10–15 minutes across town by Grab or red truck; see our transportation FAQ), and there are great options across the city.
