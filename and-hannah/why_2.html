Why Chiang Mai?

A Place We Fell In Love With

We often get asked how we ended up picking northern Thailand for our wedding! For us, Chiang Mai isn't just a travel destination—it’s a city that captured our hearts the moment we experienced its slow pace, spectacular design culture, and immense warmth.

From the peaceful early mornings along the Mae Ping River to winding through the vibrant, creative lanes of Nimman over endless cups of specialty coffee, it’s a place where we felt entirely at peace. We wanted our wedding to feel less like a formal event and more like a shared, magical holiday with the people we love most in the world. We cannot wait to show you our favorite corners of this city.
