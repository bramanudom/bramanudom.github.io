Accommodations

Staying in Chiang Mai:

While we are staying at the Anantara and hosting our main wedding day events there, please do not feel any pressure to stay at the same hotel. The city has many many great boutique options across different neighborhoods. We reccommend staying either along the scenic Ping River (close to the venue) or in Nimman neighborhood(very hip, lots of specialty coffee shops, and boutiques).

Don't worry too much about the distance between as the city center fairly compact. Even if you choose to stay in Nimman, getting across the entire city center to the river only takes about 10 to 15 minutes in a local songthaew red truck or a Grab ride (transportation FAQ).

We have secured a preferred group rate at the Anantara for anyone who wishes to stay at the venue with us (details listed below), but we want you to choose whichever neighborhood and space fit your travel style and budget best.
