Cocktail to formal! Suits? Perfect. Long dresses? Perfect. A cool formal occasion piece you've been meaning to wear? Perfect. If you're interested in renting traditional Thai clothes that's also welcome! Please that both the ceremony and reception will all be outdoors so please prioritize your comfort as well.

How do I get around Chiang Mai?

Download the Grab or Bolt app, which are basically Thai uber/lyft. It's fairly affordable (~$3-10 in the Chiang Mai city) and reliable. For a more local and fun experience, hop in a Red Truck (Songthaew) for about 30 THB per person within the city. Just hail them down and tell them where you want to go. Tuktuk's also are so fun (a little more pricey). You should ride one at least once for sure, but we don't recommend them for super long distances.

Weather in November?

November is the start of the "cool season." Days are sunny (around 80°F/30°C) and evenings are beautiful and crisp (around 65°F/18°C).
